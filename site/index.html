THE EVIDENCE MATTERS

Built on research.
Clear about the limits.

Grounded in Apple’s documentation and WWDC26. Not every custom intent is discoverable through Siri AI; availability depends on the schema, SDK, device, enabled services, and language or region.

The September 10, 2026 validation record documents four passing Swift service tests and Xcode 27 beta 6 build and metadata checks. Live Siri and system-test execution remain unverified.
